# Medical Evidence & Care Navigator

[简体中文](README.md) · [Sources](SOURCES.md) · [Clinical coverage](docs/clinical-coverage.md) · [Releases](https://github.com/dingyucanada/medical-affairs-workflows/releases/latest)

This project offers two independently installable Agent Skills.

| Skill | What it helps with |
| --- | --- |
| [Medical Health Navigation](skills/medical-health-navigation/SKILL.md) | Preview for patients, caregivers and the public. Navigate 32 care and diagnostic service routes, prepare for visits, understand test and diagnostic terms, and organize prevention, chronic follow-up or rare disease questions. |
| [Medical Affairs and Evidence Review](skills/medical-affairs-workflows/SKILL.md) | Seven workflows for pharmaceutical Medical Affairs. Covers retrieval, evidence and claim review, planning, publications, field insights, communications, and drug safety and regulatory research. |

Care Navigation helps users prepare for an encounter with their clinical team. It explains records and next steps and prioritizes emergency escalation. Clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions and approved submissions remain with responsible professionals. The detailed records and references are primarily in Simplified Chinese. Both entries accept English requests.

Download a skill ZIP from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/dingyucanada/medical-affairs-workflows/releases/latest). For Codex, extract the desired directory into the repository's `.agents/skills/` and invoke `$medical-health-navigation` or `$medical-affairs-workflows`. Follow the import instructions for other compatible clients.

> Use $medical-health-navigation to turn this de-identified report excerpt into questions to bring to my clinician.
